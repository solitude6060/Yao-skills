# Venue and schedule

Choose the venue from the topic, the contribution and the evidence, then check the dates and rules on the
venue's current call. A venue chosen from prestige or the nearest deadline alone tends to fail on fit or on
rules discovered late.

## Inputs from the current call

Read the official call for papers and author instructions, not a summary or a search snippet:

- topics and tracks, and whether the topic fits one of them;
- contribution types accepted (technique, resource, benchmark, analysis);
- page limits, and whether references and appendix count;
- abstract and full-paper dates, rebuttal window, notification date;
- anonymity rules, preprint policy, AI-use statement, reference format;
- concurrent-submission and dual-track rules, including other tracks of the same venue.

Record each date with its source and the date it was read. Generic case: a search snippet gave a rebuttal
window that disagreed with the official page; the official page was right.

## Fit check

| Question | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Does the topic match a listed area? | The area text, quoted |
| Does the contribution type match what the venue accepts? | The call's contribution types |
| Does the evidence meet the venue's maturity? | Confirmatory results ready by the deadline, or only screening results |
| Does the study use what the venue's community expects (for example, media content for a multimedia venue)? | The feasibility record |

Generic case: a multimedia venue was dropped from the fallback list once the method under study turned out to
use no media content.

## Fallbacks

- List fallbacks in order, each with its real dates, checked on its official page.
- A paper under review at one venue cannot be submitted elsewhere until its notification. A fallback whose
  deadline falls before that notification is not available for that paper.
- Two papers from one project need separate scopes; check the dual-submission rules for overlap.

Generic case: a planned fallback for a follow-up turned out to share the same deadline window as the first
choice, so it offered no second chance.

## Schedule

Work backward from the abstract deadline:

| Item | Timing |
|---|---|
| Continue or stop decision on the direction | Leaves time for confirmatory runs and writing |
| Confirmatory runs | Finished before writing freezes the claims |
| Reviewer-style internal review | Before the abstract deadline, with time to act on findings |
| Abstract registration | As the call requires |
| Full paper and submission package | Page limit checked on the compiled build |
| Rebuttal windows of papers already under review | Blocked out in the schedule |

After submission, the submitted version stays frozen: follow-up work never changes the submitted artifact,
its code release or its result rows.
